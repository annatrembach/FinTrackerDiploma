# AI Features

## AI Chat
The AI Chat allows you to manage your finances using natural language.
You can create, read, update, and delete transactions and goals by just describing what you want.

Examples:
- "I spent 50 on coffee today"
- "Add income of 3000 salary for January"
- "Show me how much I spent on food this month"
- "Create a savings goal for a new phone, target 800, by June"
- "Delete my vacation goal"

The AI understands context — you don't need to use exact commands.

## AI Analysis
The AI Analysis feature provides deep insights into your financial data:
- **Spending trends** — which categories are growing or shrinking
- **Anomalies** — unusual spikes or unexpected expenses
- **Goal comparison** — are you on track to meet your financial goals?
- **Saving tips** — personalized recommendations based on your actual data
- **Health score** — overall assessment: GOOD, MODERATE, or NEEDS_ATTENTION

To use: select a period (MONTH, QUARTER, or YEAR) and click "Analyze".

## AI Doc (this feature)
Ask any question about how the application works.
The AI searches the documentation and gives you a precise answer.

Examples:
- "How do I create a transaction?"
- "What is the difference between income and expense?"
- "How do goals work?"
- "What happens when I delete a transaction?"
